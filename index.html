Our Story

A Legacy of
Design Excellence

Founded in 2015, Hayee Interior has been at the forefront of luxury interior design in Thanjavur. Located opposite Anu Hospital, our studio has become a destination for discerning homeowners and designers seeking exceptional quality and timeless aesthetics.

We believe that every space tells a story. Our approach combines classical design principles with contemporary sensibilities, creating interiors that are both beautiful and functional.
