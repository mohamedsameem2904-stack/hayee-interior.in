Our Philosophy

The Art of Living Beautifully

At Hayee Interior, we believe that exceptional design transcends mere aesthetics. Founded in 2010 by visionary designer Isabella Hayee, our atelier has become synonymous with uncompromising quality and timeless elegance.

Every piece in our collection is a testament to the artistry of master craftsmen who have dedicated their lives to perfecting their craft. We source the finest materials from around the world—Italian marble, French velvets, Scandinavian hardwoods—and transform them into pieces that will grace your home for generations.
